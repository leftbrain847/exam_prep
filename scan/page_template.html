    What changes the plan

    A free rival already runs your model.

    open-exam-prep.com, a solo founder's AI-built catalog of 483+ exams with free notes, flashcards, practice questions and an AI tutor, covers nearly every exam here. Notes plus flashcards alone won't sell; the edge has to be video, freshness and a niche done better.

    Cheap players lock the giants.

    CPA, CFA, PMP, Security+, A+ and AWS have huge markets, but free or $15 courses (Professor Messer, Udemy, Tutorials Dojo, Coaching Actuaries) already own them.

    Spanish-language takers are underserved.

    New York's Spanish insurance pass rates are 18 to 33% versus about 46% overall, and California's Spanish contractor Law and Business exam passes only 26 to 28%. Both are large markets with thin Spanish prep.

    Exam changes open doors.

    AWS Solutions Architect Pro is being replaced, ITIL 4 is giving way to ITIL 5, CPIM got a new version in June 2026, the bar is moving to NextGen, CCNP Security got its first major blueprint revision since 2020, and ITIL 5 launched with ITIL 4 retiring at the end of 2027. Each forces incumbents to rebuild.

    Method

    1. Every row in CareerOneStop's certification file (6,484) and its state license file (580 license families) was judged on market size, stakes, existing prep spending, exam fee, how beatable the competition is, written-exam fit and liability. Nothing was excluded.

    2. Duplicates were merged (CPA appeared as 7 separate certs and state licenses), and the top 100 by that score were researched with sources: takers, pass rate, fee, named competitors and prices, and evidence of newcomers winning.

    3. Final score = 25% first-pass judgment + 75% sourced factors, averaged over the factors actually sourced. Rows with fewer than 2 sourced core factors are marked "partly verified" and ranked after verified ones; most are vendor certs (AWS, Cisco, CompTIA, Microsoft) whose owners don't publish taker counts or pass rates.

    Not used: refresh cadence (research agents scored it in opposite directions) and community size (Reddit blocked every agent). Some figures come from competitors' own pages; those are noted in each row's sources.
