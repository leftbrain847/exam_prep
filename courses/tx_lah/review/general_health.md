## Claims not confirmed (left in prose, not on cards)
- Short-term medical: the 2024 rule's 3-month initial term and 4-month total limit. The DOL 2025-08-07 statement confirms non-enforcement but does not restate these limits. The 2024 rule itself was not reopened. No card uses the month figures.
- HSA $1,000 catch-up at 55: this is set by statute (IRC Sec. 223(b)(3)) and is not in Rev. Proc. 2025-19. The statute was not reopened. The card says "set by statute".
- Textbook conventions that have no single official source and were left as "commonly": group participation of 100% (noncontributory) and 75% (contributory), the AD&D 90-day time limit, the 6-month recurrent-disability window, the 100/80/50 dental pattern, and the 60-70% DI benefit cap.
- Not reopened this session (writer's cited official sources, consistent with standard law): IRC 7702B LTC triggers, the Medicare secondary payer 20-employee rule, IRS Topic 502 7.5% AGI, TDI nonsubscriber notice rules.
