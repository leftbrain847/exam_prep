# Texas General Lines - Life, Accident and Health (InsTX-LAH05; Spanish InsTX-LAH25)
Source: Pearson VUE Texas Insurance content outlines #124401, effective 2026-09-01. 130 scored questions
(100 general knowledge + 30 Texas law), 15 pretest, 150 minutes, scaled passing score 70.
Item codes below: G = general knowledge section, S = state-specific section.

## G-I Types of policies (life) -> topic lifepolicies
A Traditional whole life: 1 ordinary whole life, 2 limited-pay and single-premium
B Interest/market-sensitive/adjustable: 1 universal, 2 variable whole, 3 variable universal, 4 interest-sensitive whole, 5 indexed
C Term: types (level, decreasing, return of premium, annually renewable); special features (renewable, convertible)
D Annuities: 1 single and flexible premium, 2 immediate and deferred, 3 fixed and variable, 4 indexed, 5 accumulation and annuity periods, 6 payout options
E Combination plans: 1 joint life (first to die), 2 survivorship (second to die)

## G-II Policy riders, provisions, options, exclusions -> topic lifeprovisions
A Riders: waiver of premium / monthly deduction, guaranteed insurability, payor benefit, AD and AD&D, term riders, other insureds, long-term care, return of premium, disability, cost of living
B Provisions and options: entire contract, insuring clause, free look, consideration, owner's rights, beneficiary designations (primary/contingent, revocable/irrevocable, common disaster, minors, by class), premium payment (modes, grace period, automatic premium loan, level or flexible), reinstatement, loans/withdrawals/partial surrenders, nonforfeiture options, dividends and dividend options (participating vs non), incontestability, assignments, suicide, misstatement of age and gender, settlement options, accelerated death benefits
D Exclusions: war, aviation, dangerous occupation

## G-III Application, underwriting, delivery + G-IX Field underwriting -> topic underwriting
A Application: required signatures, changes, incomplete applications, warranties vs representations, initial premium and receipt, replacement, point-of-sale disclosures (HIPAA, HIV consent), USA PATRIOT Act / AML, GLBA privacy
B Underwriting: insurable interest, medical information and consumer reports (MIB), Fair Credit Reporting Act, risk classification, STOLI/IOLI
C Delivery: when coverage begins, explaining policy/riders/exclusions/ratings
D Contract law: elements (consideration, offer and acceptance, competent parties, legal purpose); unique aspects (conditional, unilateral, adhesion, aleatory)
IX: completing the application, sources of insurability and HIPAA privacy (MIB, FCRA), initial premium and receipt (conditional receipt, medical exam), submitting to underwriting, delivery, explaining the policy, replacement, contract law incl. insurable interest and warranties/representations

## G-IV Retirement and other insurance concepts -> topic retirement

## G-VI Policy provisions, clauses, riders (A&H) -> topic healthprovisions
A Mandatory and optional (uniform) provisions: entire contract, time limit on certain defenses, grace period, reinstatement, notice of claim, claim forms, proof of loss, time of payment of claims, payment of claims, physical exam and autopsy, legal actions, change of beneficiary, misstatement of age or gender, change of occupation, illegal occupation, relation of earnings to insurance
B Other: insuring clause, free look, consideration, probationary period, elimination period, waiver of premium, exclusions and limitations, preexisting conditions, coinsurance, deductibles, eligible expenses, copayments, pre-authorization, usual reasonable and customary, lifetime/annual/per-cause maximums
C Riders: impairment/exclusion, guaranteed insurability, future increase option
D Renewability: noncancelable, cancelable, guaranteed renewable (plus conditionally renewable, optionally renewable as commonly tested)

## G-VII Social insurance + G-VIII Other insurance concepts -> topic socialother

VIII: total, partial, recurrent, residual disability; owner's rights; dependent children benefits; primary and contingent beneficiaries; premium modes; nonduplication and coordination of benefits; occupational vs non-occupational; tax treatment of premiums and proceeds (disability income, medical expense); managed care; workers' compensation; subrogation

## S-I Texas statutes and rules common to life and health -> topic txcommon
Commissioner powers, examinations, investigations, hearings, penalties, cease-and-desist; definitions (certificate of authority, transacting insurance, domestic/foreign/alien, stock/mutual/fraternal); licensing (agent and agency licenses, nonresident, temporary, exemptions, appointments, CE, records, denial, renewal, expiration, suspension, revocation, notices to TDI); unfair and prohibited practices (claims, false advertising, misrepresentation, defamation, rebating, fraud, boycott and coercion, commingling, unfair discrimination); agent duties incl. commission sharing; Texas Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association

## S-II Texas statutes and rules for life only -> topic txlife
Advertising and illustrations; policy summary / buyer's guide; individual life and annuity provisions (free look, grace, loans, prohibited provisions, incontestability, entire contract, premium timing, statements of insured, misstatement of age, claim settlement timing, beneficiary and assignment, reinstatement, lawsuit limits, settlement at maturity); group life (eligibility, underwriting, conversion, dependents, assignment, required provisions, accelerated benefits); credit life; replacement (purpose, definitions, agent and insurer duties); nonforfeiture law

## S-III Texas A&H only + S-IV HMOs -> topic txhealth
